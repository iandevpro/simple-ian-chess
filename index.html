History, quotes, licenses.

  1. Alex, thank you for casually mentioning that DuoLingo has a chess course
  2. Dan Heisman "Everyone's Second Chess Book", 2017. This chess engine was inspired by a paragraph from the above book, Chapter 10 "The Three Big Areas For Improvement", Safety/Tactics, p.126, where he mentioned that there is a computer chess engine, which only could look for four ply (two moves) and that it could play comparable to FIDE/USCF 1650. After reading that, I HAD TO IMPLEMENT IT MYSELF :-)
  3. "Chess is brutal", said Marcus during my first visit to San Diego Chess Club. This engine is brutal at snatching free hanging pieces. Do not leave them en prise.
  4. Initially this was a Python implementation of the negamax algorithm, later re-written in C++, now re-implemented in JS.
